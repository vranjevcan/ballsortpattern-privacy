Privacy Policy — Ball Sort Pattern

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Ball Sort Pattern is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app uses.

No account

The game does not require an account or login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any other personal information.

Game progress

Your level, record and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect the device advertising ID, approximate location (based on IP address), app interaction and diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area and the UK you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used (for example, which levels are played) and to find problems. This data is collected by Google and is not used to identify you personally.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the Google services listed above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Data deletion

Because there is no account, we do not hold any data that is linked to you. Uninstalling the app removes all game data from your device.
